Obsidian GDrive Sync

A desktop application for Linux and Windows that keeps your local Obsidian vaults in two-way sync with a folder in your own Google Drive.

What it does

  • Synchronizes a local folder you choose with a folder in your Google Drive, in both directions, using rclone bisync.
  • Detects local changes in real time and syncs them on a schedule you configure.
  • Runs entirely on your computer. There is no server: your files and your Google account credentials never pass through any third party.

Why it needs access to Google Drive

To upload your notes to your Drive folder and download changes made from other devices, the app asks you to sign in with Google and grant access to Google Drive. It only reads and writes the Drive folder you configure for each vault. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Get it

The app is open source. Download and build instructions are in the GitHub repository.
